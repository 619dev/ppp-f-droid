PaperPhonePlus is a free and open-source communication client with end-to-end encrypted private and group messaging, voice and video calls, contacts, and moments.

This F-Droid build contains no proprietary push SDK and does not require Google Play Services. Background notification delivery uses the server-provided ntfy topic when available. You can connect to a compatible self-hosted PaperPhonePlus server from the login screen.

Version 2.6.5 refreshes friendships on connection and relationship changes and adds friend request and removal controls to user profiles. Groups use signed Sender Key encryption with server-published member keys. Earlier group messages with text-appearance encryption can still be read with their original password.
